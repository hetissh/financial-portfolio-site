# Hero Patterns artwork

The SVG patterns in `src/lib/hero-patterns.json` are by **Steve Schoger**, from [Hero Patterns](https://heropatterns.com/) and the [author’s repository](https://github.com/sschoger/hero-patterns).

They are licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The catalogue was retrieved from the official website on 27 September 2026. Colours and opacity are adapted for this site's cover picker. Credit and the licence link are shown in the picker and site footer.

Only the pattern names and SVG data are bundled; the website's JavaScript is not executed or distributed. Uploaded images are user-provided assets and are not part of this catalogue.
